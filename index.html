01 Customer Experience

Patients scan a QR code to give feedback

QR codes are posted around the hospital. Patients and visitors scan one with their own phone, rate their experience and leave a comment. They don't need an app or an account.

Every concern goes to the right office, gets a named owner, and is tracked step by step until it is resolved.

  • Feedback in under a minute, from any phone
  • No complaint is lost: each one is tracked to closure
  • Satisfaction scores and trends update automatically

02 Knowledge Hub (K-Hub)

Every document in one central place

All policies, procedures and forms live in one searchable library, organized by department. Only the latest approved version is shown, and older versions are kept as history.

Staff confirm they have read a document, owners get a reminder when it is due for review, and one K-Hub account signs employees in to every other system.

  • Find any document in seconds
  • Always the current, approved version
  • See exactly who has read and acknowledged it

03 KPI Generator

Measuring every department's performance

Each department sets its key performance indicators (KPIs) and enters results every month. The system marks each one Achieved or Not achieved automatically.

A missed target needs an action plan, which is accepted by the department head and approved by the vice president. Management sees everything on one heatmap.

  • Results are classified automatically, without manual checking
  • Every missed target has an approved action plan
  • One view of performance across all departments

The same tools can work for your school, clinic or company: feedback by QR code, a central document library and KPI tracking.
